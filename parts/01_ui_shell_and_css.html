1. SDF & Surface Nets Skinning. Midnight’s plush silhouette is sampled from a smooth signed distance field of metaball lobes and extracted on the fly via Surface Nets into a watertight manifold bound by barycentric weights.

2. XPBD Co-Rotational Hexahedra & Tetrahedra. An interior volumetric lattice solves polar-decomposed hexahedral shape matching alongside 5-tetrahedra-per-cell volume conservation at 6 substeps per frame so every squish rebounds plumply.

3. Cloth Felt Ears & Rope Tail. Double-sided felt ears and a 16-node soft rope tail coupled to the spine deform independently with bending resistance, while a brass bell pendulum swings from the velvet collar.

4. Multi-Shell Kajiya-Kay Fur. Up to 30 concentric instanced shells extrude procedural tapered cones hashed in rest space, shaded with dual-lobe Kajiya-Kay fiber scattering, velvet rim sheen, comb memory, and 4x MSAA sample-mask coverage.

5. Premultiplied Shadow-Catcher Compositing. The native WebGPU canvas renders PCSS-lite contact shadows and Khronos PBR Neutral tone-mapped fur with premultiplied alpha directly over the synchronized Three.js Halloween porch.
